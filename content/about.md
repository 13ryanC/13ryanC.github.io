I'm Bryan. I'm interested in AI safety and spend a lot of my time reading, trying small experiments, and writing up what I find. This site is mostly my notes, so expect rough edges and things I'll later change my mind about.

Recently I presented a workshop poster at ICML 2026, worked on a steganography project with collaborators during the Singapore AI Safety Hub (SASH) fellowship, and I'm helping out as a mentor in the Fall 2026 [SPAR](https://sparai.org/) cohort. The [projects page](/research/) has the details.

If you spot a mistake or want to talk about something here, I'd be glad to hear from you. The links at the bottom of each page are the best way to reach me.

I'm an independent AI safety researcher. I work on preventing model misalignment, evaluating emerging risks, and developing reliable safeguards.

My recent work includes a poster at the ICML 2026 Hypothesis Testing Workshop and a study carried out during the Singapore AI Safety Hub (SASH) Fellowship. I'm also a research mentor for the Fall 2026 [SPAR](https://sparai.org/) cohort.
